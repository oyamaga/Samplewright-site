Samplewright

A free VST3 sampler and audio manipulation plugin.

About

Samplewright is a lightweight sampler designed for quick audio editing and experimentation directly inside your DAW.

Features

  • Audio file import
  • Drag and drop workflow
  • Waveform editing
  • Cut and mute
  • Reverse
  • Stretch and shrink
  • Pitch shifting
  • Undo and redo

Formats

Windows: VST3 — unsigned
macOS: VST3 Beta — unsigned / not notarized

Security Notice

The current Windows and macOS builds are not code-signed. Depending on your operating system and security settings, you may see a security warning when downloading or installing the plugin. The macOS build is also not notarized.
